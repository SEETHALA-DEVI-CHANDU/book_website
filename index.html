The Philosophy

Bridging traditional warmth with modern innovation. Co-founder of Shore and Summits.

 

Every wrong turn is part of the map. The most profound love stories feel like coming home.

My writing lives at the intersection of logic and chaos. As an AI developer, I build ordered systems. As an author, I explore what happens when those systems break—when a 'genius' is a 'disaster in love,' or when 'reckless passion' burns away all control.
I'm drawn to the "wreckage" and the "bad decisions," because that's where true growth lies. My characters must navigate the 'butterfly effect' of their choices. They have to unlearn, heal, and find 'the algorithm of letting go' before they can find their way.
It's a philosophy of imperfection. It's not about avoiding the 'broken lanes,' but about finding the courage to navigate them. Because ultimately, the goal isn't a perfect life; it's finding the person who, after all the wrong turns, still feels like home.

About Me

Where Technology Meets Storytelling

Seethala Devi Chandu hails from the small town of Ramasingavaram, Andhra Pradesh, a background that infuses his writing with a unique blend of traditional warmth and modern insight. As a Specialist Programmer at Infosys navigating the world of logic and code, he is fascinated by the beautiful illogic of human hearts. This unique blend of technical precision and creative storytelling gives him a distinct voice in contemporary romance.

As a Multi-Agent AI Systems Developer and IEEE Published Researcher, Chandu specializes in enterprise AI solutions. He architects AI systems using LangChain/LangGraph for RAI compliance automation and EU AI Act adherence, delivering an 80% reduction in manual compliance efforts. His systems process 10,000+ documents daily using MongoDB and Azure Blob Storage, transforming weeks of manual work into hours through intelligent automation.

In a world filled with casual connections and swipe-right culture, Chandu felt compelled to write stories where true love still exists and is worth finding. Tired of romantic comedies featuring impossibly wealthy heroes, he wanted to celebrate the ordinary guy - the one struggling with student loans, family expectations, and the paralyzing fear of not being enough. His stories center around everyday heroes who work regular jobs and wonder if they'll ever find someone who sees past their imperfections.

His technical expertise spans AI/ML technologies including LangChain, LangGraph, Google Gemini, Vertex AI, YOLOv8, and CNN architectures. Proficient in Python, PySpark, JavaScript, and C++, with experience across cloud platforms Azure, GCP, and AWS. His research breakthroughs in Computer Vision and Medical AI include developing YOLOv8-based corrosion detection models and CNN approaches with AlexNet architecture for knee osteoarthritis diagnostic accuracy.

Beyond his corporate life, Chandu is a budding entrepreneur and co-founder of the startup Shore and Summits. He believes that every wrong turn is a part of the map and that the most profound love stories are often the ones that feel like coming home. He writes for anyone who has ever been brilliant at everything except love.

YOLOv8-Based Model For Precise Corrosion Segmentation in Industrial Imagery

IEEE · 2024 International Conference on Computational Intelligence for Green and Sustainable Technologies (ICCIGST) · October 23, 2024

A significant risk to the longevity and intelligence of fundamental infrastructure, such as bridges and pipelines, is corrosion. This investigation offers a creative method of erosion discovery by utilizing the YOLOv8 question discovery computation. It works in tandem with the WANDB stage to facilitate efficient show planning and organization. YOLOv8's single-pass localization method handles the entire video outline in a single forward pass over the neural network, enabling real-time position optimization particularly suitable for video feeds.

Discovering Knee Osteoarthritis Using CNN Enhanced with AlexNet

IEEE · 2024 5th International Conference on Intelligent Communication Technologies and Virtual Mobile Networks (ICICV) · May 7, 2024

This research proposes a novel approach utilizing Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) with the AlexNet architecture to enhance the diagnostic accuracy and severity assessment of knee osteoarthritis. The study leverages a large dataset of knee radiographic images, encompassing diverse cases of OA progression. The AlexNet architecture, renowned for its deep learning capabilities in image classification tasks, is employed to automatically extract hierarchical features from the input radiographs, achieving significant improvements in diagnostic accuracy.
